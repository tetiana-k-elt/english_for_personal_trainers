Course Bibliography & Recommended Reading

This academic curriculum is built upon peer-reviewed exercise science, kinesiology, and sport nutrition literature. Recommended references for instructors and students include:

  • ACSM's Guidelines for Exercise Testing and Prescription (11th ed.). American College of Sports Medicine.
  • NSCA's Essentials of Personal Training (3rd ed.). National Strength and Conditioning Association.
  • Tortora, G. J., & Derrickson, B. (2020). Principles of Anatomy and Physiology. John Wiley & Sons.
  • Jeukendrup, A., & Gleeson, M. (2018). Sport Nutrition: An Introduction to Energy Production and Performance. Human Kinetics.
